When Apple's network-based speech recognition is used, information transmitted to and processed by Apple may include:

  • Live microphone audio necessary to recognize speech;

Blackthorne Groupe LLC does not receive the microphone audio transmitted to Apple's speech-recognition service and does not operate the servers used to perform Apple's network-based speech recognition.
