Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 15, 2026

Company: Blackthorne Groupe LLC

Blank Network Monitor and Blank Network Proxy do not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal information or usage data. All functionality operates locally on your device.

1. Overview

Blank Network Monitor and Blank Network Proxy are designed with privacy as a core principle. Network monitoring, traffic analysis, and proxy functionality are performed locally on your device.

2. No Data Collection

We do not collect personal information or usage data, including:

  • No names, email addresses, or account information
  • No IP addresses or device identifiers are transmitted to us
  • No analytics or tracking data
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No background data collection

3. Local Processing Only

All monitoring, analysis, and proxy functions are performed locally on your device.

  • Data is processed locally and in real-time
  • No captured traffic is transmitted to Blackthorne Groupe LLC
  • No data is sent to external processing servers
  • No remote monitoring occurs

Depending on the application and features enabled by the user, the software may display information such as domain names, DNS activity, IP addresses, connection metadata, network traffic statistics, HTTP requests, HTTPS requests, or other network-related information. This information is processed locally and is not collected or transmitted by the Company.

4. Optional HTTPS Inspection

Blank Network Proxy may include optional HTTPS inspection functionality.

  • HTTPS inspection is disabled unless explicitly enabled by the user
  • Users must manually install and trust a local certificate before HTTPS inspection can occur
  • HTTPS inspection occurs entirely on-device
  • No decrypted traffic is transmitted to Blackthorne Groupe LLC

5. Data Storage

The applications do not automatically transmit or back up monitored network data.

  • Network activity is displayed locally during application use
  • Data may be retained locally when a user explicitly saves a session or exports data
  • Any saved or exported data remains under the user's control
  • Blackthorne Groupe LLC does not have access to locally stored sessions or exports

6. Data Sharing

We do not share any user data with third parties.

  • No service providers receive captured traffic data
  • No analytics platforms are used
  • No advertising networks are used
  • No third-party monitoring services are used

7. Exported Data

The applications may allow users to export network information.

  • Exporting is strictly user-initiated
  • Exported data remains entirely under the user's control
  • The applications do not automatically transmit exported data
  • Users are responsible for the storage and sharing of exported files

8. Security

Because network data is processed locally and not transmitted to Blackthorne Groupe LLC, exposure risk is minimized. Users are responsible for securing their devices and any files they choose to save or export.

9. Children's Privacy

The applications do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date on this page.
